2.2 Data Sources and Management

In Dusk, geography is built from real OpenStreetMap tiles over IIT Guwahati (EPSG:32646); all baseline street condition values in v1 come from synthetic_generator.

Where Our Data Comes From

  • OSM Road Network: 9 bounding-box tiles over IIT Guwahati split into 1,966 walkable ~30 m segments.
  • Synthetic Baseline: Deterministic seeded scenario (scenario_v1.json) across 5 campus zones and 12 night hours.
  • Community Reports: User-submitted outage, blockage, dog pack, or waterlogging reports with Hinglish LLM/replay extraction.

Where We Store It

  • SQLite Database (fnn.db): Stores segments, hourly observations, collection catalog, reports, and condition events.
  • Source Priority Ranking: reviewed_observation (3) > scenario_event (2) > synthetic_generator (1).
  • Strict Non-Dependence: No v1 layer depends on institutional permissions (CCTV and crime records are rejected).

How Dusk Uses the Data

  • Pessimistic Unknowns: Missing fields default to 0.70 without renormalizing; all-missing segments score 1.0 (no_data).
  • Continuity Search: Binary search + product graph minimizes the longest continuous concerning stretch (> τ = 0.5) within 1.4× fastest time.
  • Verified Loop: Only reviewer-verified reports create expiring whole-edge condition events that trigger rerouting.
